PRIVACY POLICY

Rustbore for iPhone · Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Rustbore does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your game progress (your world, upgrades, cash and settings) and your personal records (split times and a log of past runs) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.
  • No network requests: the game runs entirely offline.

Children

Because Rustbore collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of this (for example, adding optional online features), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
