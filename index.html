RUSTBORE

Dig until something digs back. A mining game for iPhone.

Two hundred years after the Hush, you find an old bore-rig in a collapsed garage. Drill down through the ruins of our world, then through the remains of civilisations nobody remembers. Sell what you find, upgrade the rig, and keep going. Something is asleep at the bottom.

FAQ

How do I drive?
Touch anywhere and drag: a joystick appears under your thumb. Push up to fire the jets, push down (or into a wall) to drill.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on your iPhone. The game saves itself whenever you visit a building or get towed home, and you can save any time at the Bunkhouse. Deleting the app deletes your save and records.
I keep getting hurt when I land.
Long free-falls dent the hull. Ease off the jets gradually to land softly, and repair at Patch & Weld.
What does Impossible mode do?
Everything is harder, and there is no tow truck: if your rig is destroyed, the run ends and the save is erased. Your records are kept.
Does Rustbore collect any data?
No. See the privacy policy.
